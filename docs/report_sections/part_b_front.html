DECLARATION


I hereby declare that the capstone engineering project work entitled "Real Estate Marketplace Platform" is an authentic record of my own project development work completed under the academic framework of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Gulzar Group of Institutes, Ludhiana, Punjab.

The software platform was designed, developed, integrated, and tested independently by me during the 8th semester of the Bachelor of Technology program. All core modules—including JWT session authentication, Twilio and Nodemailer dual-channel OTP verification, role-based access middleware guards, the 5-step property listing wizard, comparative grid filters, leaflet map geocodes, Socket.IO chat setups, and browser-side CryptoJS E2E message encryption—were implemented by me.

I further declare that this project work has not previously formed the basis for the award of any other degree, diploma, fellowship, or similar academic title at this or any other university. Wherever technical concepts, libraries, third-party packages, or open-source solutions have been integrated, they have been cited and referenced appropriately.
